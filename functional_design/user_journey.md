Emma realizes that many of her favorite moments with friends and family are starting to blur together. She recalls a trip she took a few years ago but struggles to remember the details—who was there, what they did, and the little moments that made it special. She feels frustrated that her memories are fading and distorting over time, and she wishes she had a way to preserve them more vividly.  
She downloads Myria after hearing about it from a friend. She begins by creating her profile and adding a few recent memories: a dinner with friends, a weekend hike, and a family birthday celebration, each with photos and short reflections. These entries are placed into her timeline, giving her a clear view of her memories.  
Emma is soon invited to a shared group with her close friends. Together, they begin tracking their everyday moments—weekend hangouts, trips, and casual snapshots. They add memories to their shared timeline that each of them can contribute to. The group timeline grows into a collective record of their friendship, helping to keep their shared history alive. From there, Emma explores filters that let her control which memories appear on her personal timeline, making it easy to focus on memories tied to specific people.  
At the end of the year, Emma tries Myria’s AI summary assistant. It generates a personalized recap of her most meaningful moments, highlighting recurring themes and special events. She smiles as she reflects on the year, reassured that her memories are preserved in detail and strengthened by the connections she shares with others.
