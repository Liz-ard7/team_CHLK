# Design Summary

Our memory-collection platform (Myria) is designed to help individuals and groups preserve meaningful experiences in a fun and collaborative way. Memory is priceless but easily faded. Therefore, our design provides a structured digital space where memories can be intentionally recorded, organized, and revisited with loved ones.

The design integrates four core components. User Authentication ensures that each person has a secure and persistent identity, protecting their personal data and memory ownership. Groups create controlled collaborative spaces where users can invite others, contribute together, and make collective decisions, including democratic member removal to prevent abuse. Memory Entries allow users to record experiences through text and images, where creators retain control over editing and deletion while still enabling others to contribute. Image Storage supports secure uploading and viewing through signed URLs, ensuring both safety and accessibility of media that often holds emotional significance.

Ethical concerns raised in our analysis, such as consent, privacy, power imbalance, and data security, are directly addressed through invitation-based participation, creator ownership of memories, democratic group governance, and controlled access to images. We explored this tension extensively by balancing democratic group control with individual ownership and ultimately arrived at a compromise that respects both collective decision-making and creator rights. These features help ensure that collaboration remains consensual, safe, and respectful.

Some questions remain open, including long-term data retention policies, moderation of harmful or disputed content, and how to handle conflicts over shared memories. The worst-case scenario we discussed is when a group of users were friends at the time a memory was created, but later had a falling-out. In that situation, it becomes unclear what should happen to the shared memories they created together. Despite these unresolved areas, the design establishes a strong, ethically grounded foundation for a platform that helps people preserve and share the memories that shape their lives.
