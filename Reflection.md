## Lizzy

I learned how incredibly quickly app development can go! I was slightly apprehensive at the deadlines-- I'm used to big projects taking months upon months of work. But I still felt that we were able to complete our tasks on time. I'm fascinated by Context, I think it is a very interesting tool with interesting intricacies, like the fact that if certain linked Markdown files are ordered differently, it impacts their relative order of importance. I liked how we created our ideas as well-- I thought my "Memory" proposal domain was relatively rare until a bunch of us had similar ideas, which was interesting to learn about. It was the first time I've worked on a team where we've had to design ideas together, not just be given a task to accomplish, which was a good experience! It was really cool to see how our ideas all developed together, and how it shaped the end product. I felt like I learned a lot about development-- not just in how to create an app but how to make an app that people actually want to use, one that's helpful for them. I do wish we had a bit more time, though, I think there were some things we could've improved, like speeding up some of the login stuff and optimizing slightly.

## Kelly

Overall, I think that this project went pretty smoothly. At first, I was a bit worried as we somewhat struggled to find a time during which all of us could meet to decide on what exactly our app was going to be about, but this scheduling issue did not persist for too long. Additionally, I feel that our group did well in task division, which made independent (but still structured) work possible. Regarding our actual app itself, I am satisfied with our idea. We didn't really have any debate with regards to choosing the memory domain. There were a few different ideas for features within this domain, but I believe we narrowed down the features in a way that balanced originality with feasibility.

The only part of the project that I was less satisfied with was the timeline of the project, specifically the short time interval between deadlines. While I understand that this project had multiple people working on it compared to our first project, I felt that these deadlines (mainly the earlier ones for app design, not implementation) could have been spaced out better to give a bit more leeway especially as scheduling difficulties are a common occurrence in group projects. The timing of the beta checkpoint requiring people to work over the holiday break also felt a bit unfortunate.
